Frequently Asked Questions

Is this actually free? What's the catch?

It's free. No hidden cost, no card, nothing to cancel later. Here is why. You don't know me, so there's no reason for you to take my word that I can help you. I'd rather prove it. If your dating life is visibly better in 90 days, I'd ask for a testimonial and maybe a referral. That's the whole deal.

Will I be pressured to buy something on the call?

No, because there's nothing to buy. The call is me working out whether I can actually help you, and you working out whether you want my help. If either answer is no, we leave it there.

What happens on the call, and how long does it take?

45 minutes. We look at where your dating life is now and where you want it to be, I identify the biggest things standing in the way, and we build a plan for what can realistically be fixed inside 90 days.

What do you want in return?

Two things: do the work, and give me honest feedback along the way. If you get results at the end, an honest testimonial in whatever format you're comfortable with. If you don't get results, you owe me nothing, and I'd still want to hear how I could have helped you more/better.

Will anyone find out I did this?

Only if you want them to. What you tell me stays between us. If you're happy at the end and want to give a testimonial, you pick the format: first name only, face blurred, voice only, or full name and video. Your choice, and it isn't a condition of working together.

Is this pickup artist stuff?

No. No routines, no scripts, no lines to memorise. The work is on the things that actually move the needle: how you look, how you present yourself, how you communicate, and what you believe about yourself. Nothing in this program would embarrass you if a woman saw it.

How much of my time does this take?

Expect around 8 hours a week after onboarding, and most of it is doing things you already do, differently, rather than adding tasks to your day. Some changes take an afternoon. Others, like getting in shape, run past the 90 days.

Can you guarantee results?

Yes, and here's exactly how. I won't promise a specific outcome sight-unseen. That gets set once I see your actual starting point on Day 1. From there we agree on a personalized target together. Hit it within 90 days and all you owe me is a testimonial and a referral. Don't hit it, and you get a $100 Amazon gift card while I keep working with you for free until you do. See "My Guarantee" above for the full details.

How is this different from the free advice on the internet?

Three things
1. It's built around your specific situation rather than a general audience, which is the difference between a bespoke suit and one off the rack.

2. You get my personal number, so you can text me when something unexpected comes up.

3. I don't teach tricks or tactics: the framework is logic based, built out of dating hundreds of women across more than twelve countries.

What happens after the 90 days?

We look at what actually changed. From there you either keep going on your own with what you've learned, or we talk about continuing with my hands on guidance. Continuing would be a paid arrangement. There's no obligation either way, and the 90 days are yours regardless.

What do I need to do to get started?

Take the free Attraction Scorecard below the video. It takes about three minutes and shows you which of the 7 pillars is holding you back. If it looks like a fit, you'll be able to book a call at a time that suits you.

I'm recently divorced. Will this work for me?

Yes, and you're starting from a better place than you think. You already know you can attract a woman, which a lot of men don't. And you've learned what not to do, which is often worth more than knowing what to do.

I've never been in a relationship. Will this work for me?

Yes. In that case there are usually only two or three real bottlenecks holding you back, and some of them are fixable well inside 90 days.

I'm over 40, short, balding, or not making much money. Am I wasting my time?

Probably not. Almost none of what holds men back is fixed at birth, and the parts that are matter far less than how you present what you've got. The only thing I can't work around is a man who won't do the work.

Will this help me get the specific woman I like?

I can't promise you one particular person. The goal is to make more women find you attractive by focusing on improving the things that matter to most women. Whether she's one of them isn't in my control, and being fixated on a single woman is usually a bottleneck of its own.
