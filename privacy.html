Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information The Sovereign Doctrine collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using the site or submitting the Attraction Scorecard you agree to it.

What we collect

When you complete the Attraction Scorecard we collect what you enter:

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Your answers to the scorecard questions, including your age, work situation, how soon you want to start, your dating history and goals, and anything you choose to write in the optional free text box.
  • Your scorecard result, which the site calculates from your answers.

If you book a call, Calendly collects the details you give it, such as your name, email and the time you choose. Like any website, our hosting provider also records technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you load a page.

Some of your answers are personal. Please only share what you are comfortable sharing. The free text box is optional.

How we use it

  • To show you your scorecard result.
  • To decide whether a call is a good fit and to prepare for it.
  • To contact you about your result, your booking, and the coaching programme.
  • To keep the site working and to improve it.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who handles it

We use trusted services to run the site. Your information is processed by:

  • Google (Google Sheets), where scorecard submissions are stored.
  • Telegram, which sends the site owner a notification when you submit the scorecard.
  • Calendly, which handles call bookings under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts and delivers the website.

These providers may process data outside your country. If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, such as Google Analytics or social media pixels, we will update this page and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to follow up with you and run the service. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to how we use it, or ask for a copy. To make a request, reply to any email you have received from us and tell us what you would like, and we will respond promptly.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online service is completely secure.

Who this site is for

This site is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
